# References

## Joint-embedding predictive architectures

- Assran et al. (2023), [Self-Supervised Learning from Images with a Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.08243), and the [official I-JEPA implementation](https://github.com/facebookresearch/ijepa).
- Bardes et al. (2024), [Revisiting Feature Prediction for Learning Visual Representations from Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08471).
- Assran et al. (2025), [V-JEPA 2: Self-Supervised Video Models Enable Understanding, Prediction and Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985), and the [official implementation](https://github.com/facebookresearch/vjepa2).
- Melo et al. (2024), [T-JEPA: Augmentation-Free Self-Supervised Learning for Tabular Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05016), and its [reference implementation](https://github.com/jose-melo/t-jepa).
- Verdenius et al. (2024), [LaT-PFN: A Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture for Time-Series Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.10093), and its [reference implementation](https://github.com/StijnVerdenius/Lat-PFN).
